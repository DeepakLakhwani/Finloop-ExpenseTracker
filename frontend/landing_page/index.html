FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Is FinLoop completely free to use?

Yes! FinLoop offers full access to expense tracking, category budgeting, PDF/CSV statement exports, and encrypted Google Drive backups for free.

How safe is my backup data on Google Drive?

All backups are encrypted directly on your device using AES-256 encryption before uploading. Only you hold the decryption key linked to your account.

Which languages does FinLoop support?

FinLoop supports 10+ major languages including English, Spanish, Hindi, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Portuguese, Arabic, and Russian.

Can I export my data to Excel or PDF?

Yes, you can export customized transaction date ranges directly into formatted PDF statements or CSV spreadsheets anytime.
